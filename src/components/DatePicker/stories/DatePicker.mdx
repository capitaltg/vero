<DocsWhenToUse>

<DocsWhenToUseCard kind="use">

- **To schedule or record an event**, such as an appointment, where seeing a calendar helps.
- **When the day of the week matters**, such as picking a weekday for a delivery.
- **For dates close to today.** The calendar opens on the selected date, or on today's date, so
  nearby dates take a few key presses or clicks.
- For a start and an end date, use a
  [DateRangePicker](?path=/docs/data-display-daterangepicker--docs).

</DocsWhenToUseCard>

<DocsWhenToUseCard kind="avoid">

- **Dates users know or can look up**, such as a date of birth or a passport's issue date: use text
  fields, such as separate month, day and year [Inputs](?path=/docs/inputs-forms-input--docs).
  Paging back decades in a calendar is slow.
- **The day of the week doesn't matter:** text fields are usually quicker.
- **Users need to type the date.** Vero's date picker is pick-only: there's no text field to type
  into. USWDS recommends always letting users type a date, because some people find that faster or
  easier. Where that matters, use text fields with a format hint, such as "MM/DD/YYYY".

</DocsWhenToUseCard>

</DocsWhenToUse>

<DocsSubheading id="usability">Usability guidance</DocsSubheading>

- **Say what the date is for in a visible label**, such as "Appointment date". See
  [Your responsibility](#accessibility-your-responsibility) for how to link it.
- **Use the placeholder as an instruction, not as the label.** It's replaced by the date once one
  is picked.
- **Limit the choices with `minDate`, `maxDate` and `excludeDates`** rather than rejecting a date
  after it's picked. Unavailable days are disabled in the calendar.
- **Explain the limits in hint text**, such as "Weekdays in the next 30 days". The calendar doesn't
  say why a day is unavailable.
- **Set `minDate` and `maxDate` for dates far from today.** They also set the years in the
  calendar's year list. Without them, the list covers this year and the 23 before it, so a future
  year can only be reached month by month.
- **Don't submit the form or start another action when a date is picked.** Users may pick the
  wrong day, and screen reader users can lose their place.
- **Disable the picker only when no date can be chosen**, and say how to make it available.
- **The picked date is shown with the month name**, such as "March 14, 2024", so it can't be
  misread as day-month or month-day.

</DocsSection>

  intro="What the date picker handles for you and what you need to add."

<DocsSubheading id="built-in">Built in</DocsSubheading>

- **The trigger is a button** with `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`. Its text, the
  placeholder or the picked date, is its name unless you label it.
- **Opening the calendar moves focus into it**, to the selected day, or to today when no date is
  picked. Focus stays in the calendar until it closes.
- **Keyboard in the calendar**, as in the
  [WAI-ARIA date picker example](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/examples/datepicker-dialog/):
  - Left and Right arrows move by day, Up and Down by week.
  - Home and End move to the start and end of the week.
  - Page Up and Page Down move by month; with Shift, by year.
  - Enter or Space picks the focused day, closes the calendar and returns focus to the trigger.
  - Escape closes the calendar and returns focus to the trigger.
  - Tab moves between the previous and next month buttons, the month and year buttons, and the
    days.
- **The calendar is a grid** (`role="grid"`) named by its month and year. Each day is a button
  named with its full date, such as "Thursday, March 14th, 2024". The selected day adds
  "selected" and is marked `aria-selected`; today's date starts with "Today".
- **Unavailable days are disabled**, and the arrow keys skip them.
- **When disabled, the trigger stays focusable** and is marked `aria-disabled`, so screen reader
  users can still find it. Clicks, Enter and Space don't open the calendar.
- **It passes your `aria-describedby` and `aria-labelledby` to the trigger.** In a
  [FormItem](?path=/docs/inputs-forms-formitem--docs), the hint and error are added to your own
  `aria-describedby`, and an error sets `aria-invalid`.
- **Contrast:** the trigger's border is 6.7:1 against white and the placeholder text 5.3:1. The
  selected day's fill and the focus ring on days are 6.7:1. Days from the next and previous months
  are 4.5:1.

<DocsSubheading id="your-responsibility">Your responsibility</DocsSubheading>

- **Give it a label that includes the date.** A plain `<label for>`, such as FormItem's string
  `label`, replaces the trigger's text as its name, so screen reader users hear "Start date" but
  not the picked date ([#225](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues/225)). Point
  `aria-labelledby` at the label and at the trigger itself:

  ```tsx
  <FormItem
    hintText="Weekdays in the next 30 days."
    label={
      <Label htmlFor="start" id="start-label">
        Start date
      </Label>
    }
  >
    <DatePicker
      aria-labelledby="start-label start"
      id="start"
      maxDate={maxDate}
      minDate={minDate}
      value={start}
      onChange={setStart}
    />
  </FormItem>
  ```

  The trigger's name is then "Start date Pick a date", and "Start date" plus the date once one is
  picked.

- **Validate required dates yourself.** For now, `required` doesn't stop the form from
  submitting, and its `aria-required` isn't announced on a button
  ([#224](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues/224)). Put "(required)" in the label text, check
  for a date on submit, and show the error with FormItem's `errorText`.
- **Explain unavailable dates in hint text.** Disabled days are skipped and not explained.
- **Make the label say what the date is for.** For now, the calendar dialog has no name of its own
  ([#220](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues/220)), so screen readers announce only "dialog"
  when it opens.
- **Expect the month and year buttons to be hard to reach by voice.** For now, their names are
  "Select calendar month" and "Select calendar year", not the month and year they show
  ([#223](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues/223)). The previous and next month buttons and
  the arrow keys still work.
- **Show the picked date outside the calendar where users rely on Windows High Contrast mode.**
  For now, the selected day isn't visible in the calendar in that mode
  ([#222](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues/222)). The trigger still shows the date as text.
- **Offer typed entry where users need it.** The picker has no text field. Some users, including
  some screen reader and voice users, find typing a date easier: use text fields instead, as in
  [When to use something else](#guidance-when-to-use-something-else).
- **Test it in your page.** Run the
  [USWDS date picker accessibility tests](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/date-picker/accessibility-tests/)
  on your own form, including a screen reader pass. They're written for USWDS's typed-entry
  picker, so skip the steps for the text field.

For an accessibility review, see
[all open accessibility issues for DatePicker and DateRangePicker](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aa11y+%28in%3Atitle+DatePicker+OR+in%3Atitle+DateRangePicker%29).
