<DocsSubheading id="when-to-use">When to use</DocsSubheading>

- **Users need only a few parts of a long page**, such as a list of FAQs where each reader looks
  for one answer.
- **There's a lot of content and little space**, and each section makes sense on its own.

<DocsSubheading id="when-to-use-something-else">When to use something else</DocsSubheading>

- **Users need to read most or all of the content:** show it as plain text with clear headings.
  Hiding it adds a click for each section and makes users guess where things are.
- **There isn't much content:** don't condense it. A short page doesn't need an accordion.
- **Users switch between views of the same thing**, such as Details and History: use
  [Tabs](?path=/docs/actions-navigation-tabs--docs).
- **Users need to find text with the browser's find in page:** a closed section's content isn't
  rendered, so find in page can't match it.

<DocsSubheading id="usability">Usability guidance</DocsSubheading>

- **Write specific titles.** "How to renew a passport" tells users what's inside; "More
  information" doesn't. The title is the only thing users see before they open a section.
- **Let users open several sections when they might compare them.** Use `mode="multiple"`. Use
  `mode="single"` when the sections are steps or alternatives and one at a time keeps the page
  short.
- **Keep `isCollapsible` on** unless a section must always be open. With it off, users can't close
  the open section, only switch to another one.
- **Leave space between the triggers and anything clickable in the content**, so users don't close a
  section by mistake.
- **Don't rely on the variant's color.** `success`, `danger` and `warning` color the triggers, but
  the color isn't announced and some users can't see it. If a section's status matters, say it in
  the title.

  intro="What the accordion handles for you and what you need to add."

<DocsSubheading id="built-in">Built in</DocsSubheading>

- **It follows the [WAI-ARIA accordion pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/accordion/).**
  Each title is a native `<button>` inside a heading (`h3` unless you set `headingLevel`), so
  screen reader users can jump between sections by heading.
- **Each button announces whether its section is open** with `aria-expanded`. An open section's
  button points to its panel with `aria-controls`.
- **Each panel is a `region` named by its button**, through `aria-labelledby`. Closed panels are
  hidden and their content isn't rendered, so screen readers skip them.
- **Keyboard:** Tab moves through the buttons and any focusable content in open panels. Enter or
  Space opens or closes a section. Up and Down Arrow move between the buttons, wrapping at the ends,
  and Home and End go to the first and last.
- **With `isCollapsible` off**, the open section's button is marked `aria-disabled`, so screen
  readers announce that it can't be closed. It stays focusable.
- **Focus is visible on every variant:** a 3px blue ring with a white gap. In Windows High Contrast
  mode an outline shows instead, and the caret stays visible. The caret is hidden from screen
  readers, since `aria-expanded` already gives the state.
- **Contrast:** the title text is at least 4.6:1 against the trigger on every variant (`success` and
  `danger` are the lowest), and the hover colors of the colored variants are darker still.

<DocsSubheading id="your-responsibility">Your responsibility</DocsSubheading>

- **Set `headingLevel` to fit your page.** It defaults to `h3`. Use the level after the heading the
  accordion sits under, so the page's outline doesn't skip a level.
- **Keep titles to text.** The title is the button's content and its accessible name. Don't put
  links, buttons or headings in it. An icon in a custom title needs `aria-hidden` or a text
  alternative.
- **Keep the accordion short when many sections can be open.** Each open panel is a landmark
  region, and in `multiple` mode a long accordion can fill the screen reader's list of landmarks.
- **Check the contrast of custom colors.** Text in `triggerClassName` and `contentClassName` needs
  4.5:1 against its background, and the focus ring needs to show against the trigger.
- **Test it in your page.** Run the
  [USWDS accordion accessibility tests](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/accordion/accessibility-tests/)
  on your own page, including a screen reader pass.

For an accessibility review, see
[all open accessibility issues for Accordion](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aa11y+in%3Atitle+Accordion).
