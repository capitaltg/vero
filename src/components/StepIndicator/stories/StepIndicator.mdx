<DocsWhenToUse>

<DocsWhenToUseCard kind="use">

- **For a form or process split across pages** that groups into three or more high-level steps.
- **When users move through the steps in order**, with back and next buttons.

</DocsWhenToUseCard>

<DocsWhenToUseCard kind="avoid">

- **The number of steps can change** based on the user's answers: a step indicator that grows or
  shrinks is confusing. Show only the heading, or no indicator.
- **Users can complete steps in any order:** use a list of sections with their status, or
  [Tabs](?path=/docs/actions-navigation-tabs--docs).
- **A short form with fewer than three steps:** leave it out.
- **Showing where a page sits in a site:** use
  [Breadcrumbs](?path=/docs/actions-navigation-breadcrumbs--docs).

</DocsWhenToUseCard>

</DocsWhenToUse>

<DocsSubheading id="usability">Usability guidance</DocsSubheading>

- **Keep step labels short**, one or two words, such as "Profile" or "Review". Keep any
  `description` to a few words too.
- **Provide navigation separately.** The step indicator isn't interactive: put back and next
  buttons below the form, as in the interactive example.
- **Keep the default `variant`** for the USWDS look, with a segment per step. The `counter`
  variant shows numbered circles, with a check on completed steps.
- **Set `headingLevel`** so the "Step X of Y" heading fits your page's outline. It defaults to
  `h4`.
- **Expect only the heading on narrow screens.** Below 768px, the `default` variant hides its
  segments and labels and shows only the heading.
- **Use `showCurrentAsCompleted`** on a final confirmation page, so every step shows as done.

</DocsSection>

<DocsSubheading id="built-in">Built in</DocsSubheading>

- **Each step's status is in text, not only color.** Each step has visually hidden text saying
  it's "completed" or "not completed", and the current step is marked `aria-current="step"`.
- **The steps are an ordered list** named "Steps", inside a region named "Step indicator". Pass
  your own `aria-label` to rename the region.
- **The heading reads "Step 2 of 4 Profile"** to screen readers: a visually hidden "Step" comes
  before the number. Its level is set by `headingLevel`.
- **Nothing in it is focusable.** It shows progress only, so keyboard users don't tab through
  it.
- **On narrow screens**, the `default` variant's list is hidden from screen readers as well as
  visually, so only the heading is announced.
- **Contrast:** labels are 17.2:1 against white for completed steps, 6.7:1 for the current step
  and 5.3:1 for steps not started. The current and completed segments are 6.7:1 and 9.6:1.
  Segments for steps not started are light gray (1.25:1), but their status is also given in text.

<DocsSubheading id="your-responsibility">Your responsibility</DocsSubheading>

- **Set `headingLevel`** to fit the headings around it. The default `h4` is rarely right.
- **Tell users the step changed.** When users move to the next step, update the page's `<title>`
  (for example, "Profile – step 2 of 4") and move focus to the step's heading or the top of the
  form, so screen reader users know where they are.
- **Prefer the `counter` variant where users rely on Windows High Contrast mode.** For now, the
  `default` variant's segments disappear in that mode
  ([#219](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues/219)). The labels and the heading stay.
- **Keep labels short.** For now, screen readers read each step's label twice when labels are
  shown ([#221](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues/221)).
- **Give each step indicator a distinct `aria-label`** if a page has more than one.
- **Test it in your page.** Run the
  [USWDS step indicator accessibility tests](https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/step-indicator/accessibility-tests/)
  on your own form, including a screen reader pass.

For an accessibility review, see
[all open accessibility issues for StepIndicator](https://github.com/capitaltg/vero/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aa11y+in%3Atitle+StepIndicator).
